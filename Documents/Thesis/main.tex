\begin{abstract}
This master thesis presents the concept and implementation of a Type3 \ac{AAS} with integration into a Multi-Agent System (MAS) using the Belief-Desire-Intention (BDI) approach. The proposed proactive AAS framework aims to enhance the interoperability and autonomy of industrial assets by leveraging agent-based technologies. The thesis details the design principles, architecture, and implementation strategies employed in developing the AAS, along with a comprehensive evaluation of its performance in a simulated industrial environment. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of the BDI-based MAS integration in enabling dynamic decision-making and adaptive behavior in response to changing operational conditions.
\end{abstract}
