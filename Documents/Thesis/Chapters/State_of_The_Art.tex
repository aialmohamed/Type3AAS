\section{State of the art}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!5!white,colframe=yellow!75!black,
  title=About this chapter]
  This chapter provides a comprehensive review of the current state of the art in the fields of Asset Administration Shells (AAS), Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), and their integration. It discusses existing frameworks, methodologies, and technologies relevant to the development of Type3 AAS with BDI agent integration. The chapter identifies gaps in the current research landscape, setting the stage for the proposed contributions of this thesis.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Current frameworks for Asset Administration Shells}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!5!white,colframe=yellow!75!black,
  title=About Current Frameworks for AAS]
  This section reviews the existing frameworks and standards for Asset Administration Shells (AAS). It discusses various implementations, their features, and how they address the challenges of interoperability and asset management in Industry 4.0. The section also highlights the limitations of current frameworks, particularly in relation to Type3 AAS and their integration with intelligent systems.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Current frameworks for Muti Agent Systems and BDI Agents}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!5!white,colframe=yellow!75!black,
  title=About Current Frameworks for MAS and BDI Agents]
  This section reviews the existing frameworks and methodologies for Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) and Belief-Desire-Intention (BDI) agents. It covers various agent architectures, communication protocols, and decision-making models used in industrial applications. The section also discusses the challenges and limitations of current MAS and BDI frameworks, especially in the context of their integration with Asset Administration Shells (AAS).
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Existing integrations of AAS with MAS}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!5!white,colframe=yellow!75!black,
  title=About Existing Integrations of AAS with MAS]
  This section explores the current state of integration between Asset Administration Shells (AAS) and Multi-Agent Systems (MAS). It reviews existing approaches, case studies, and implementations that combine AAS with MAS to enhance asset management and automation. The section identifies the strengths and weaknesses of these integrations, providing insights into the potential for incorporating BDI agents within Type3 AAS frameworks.
\end{tcolorbox}
