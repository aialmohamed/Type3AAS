\chapter{Concept}
\label{ch:concept}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=green!5!white,colframe=green!75!black,
  title=About this chapter]
  This chapter presents the core concepts underlying the proposed Type3 Asset Administration Shell (AAS) framework and its integration with Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) using the Belief-Desire-Intention (BDI) model. It details the architectural design, key components, and interaction mechanisms that facilitate proactive behavior in industrial assets. The chapter also discusses the theoretical foundations and practical considerations involved in implementing the AAS-MAS integration.
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=green!5!white,colframe=green!75!black,
